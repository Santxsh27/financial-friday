Open Financial Friday

Your next money decision.
A clearer way forward.

Friday reads a supported INR bank or card statement, checks its source evidence, organises the reviewed history, and helps explain what deserves your attention. No bank connection, payment or screenshot collection is required.

First: build a goal roadmap

Open Goals. Use this artificial example:

I want a travel savings fund of INR 120000 in 12 months. Monthly income INR 50000, living costs excluding debt INR 30000, monthly debt payments INR 0, liquid savings INR 70000, protected buffer INR 50000.

  1. Paste the example in Friday's message box on Goals and send. Allow Gemini goal processing beside that box; the pending request then resumes automatically. Goal tools → Edit numbers works without AI.
  2. Answer only missing questions in that same chat. The ring, milestones and projected path appear automatically. Expect INR 20000 starting pot and INR 8333.34 monthly step; exact assumptions are collapsed.
  3. Save my roadmap, tick confirmation and save. Reload: your private roadmap restores.
  4. I saved some money → 1000 → Review update → confirm. The reported pot becomes INR 21000. My savings updates → Undo corrects the report.
  5. Ask Friday about your goal with consent enabled. It uses roadmap totals, not saved statements, and cannot edit or pay.
  6. Try a savings target or chosen emergency reserve, not only a purchase. Replacing the one active priority requires confirmation. Export includes archives. Advanced is a separate unsaved multi-purchase/loan calculator.

Only additional money actually reported as set aside changes progress. Passing time, imports and reduced spending never count automatically. No bank balance, future returns or financial stability are guaranteed.

In chat, choose Current info, review a public query, confirm Google Search processing, then Search & show here. Expect inline source links and Search Suggestions, not a live ticker or personalised investment recommendation. No private statements are sent.

Then: try a statement in five steps

  1. Sign in with Google. Top-right My account shows which account is active. A returning browser may reuse Google sign-in. Each visitor has a separate workspace.
  2. Download one artificial sample below. The five CSVs represent the same activity in different layouts. Do not combine them as different accounts.
  3. Home → Add a statement. Select the file, use a nickname such as Sample HDFC, leave Bank account selected, confirm processing, then choose Read my statement.
  4. Review before saving. Check four transactions and the totals below. Open Source checks to see both agreement and missing evidence. Confirm the rows and choose Save & see my spending.
  5. Explore, then ask Friday. Open All entries and use Back. In chat choose Statements ready · tap to share, enable statement totals, then ask a question. To test another file, choose Statements → Start a new review; old imports are preserved and can be restored.

For a richer review queue, use Add a statement → Try with an example inside Friday. That example has 35 artificial entries across three months. Questions are sent only when you press Send. Sharing statement totals with chat and generating a spoken briefing require separate consent.

Optional voice conversation

Talk to Friday is beside chat and available as a small floating control on other pages. Confirm Google audio processing, allow the microphone, then record a short question. Stop & transcribe places the words in your message box: check them, then send. Type instead works without microphone access. Speak short replies enables Google narration of displayed reply text; Stop audio stops playback. No wake word, always-on listening, recording storage by Friday or bank actions. My money also retains an optional aggregate spoken briefing.

Limits, privacy and troubleshooting

CSV: 1 MB / 500 rows; PDF: 4 MB / six pages / 200 rows. INR only. Supported headings and four-digit years are required for these CSV adapters. Unknown financial fields are rejected without a partial import. Unlock PDFs locally; never enter their passwords, PINs, OTPs or card secrets here.

CSV reading is deterministic. PDF extraction uses Gemini on Vertex AI; scans require configured Document AI. Imported statements are user-supplied evidence, not authenticated bank feeds. Raw files are not archived; reviewed normalised rows are stored after confirmation. Sharing totals does not share transaction descriptions. Use My account → Privacy for data controls.

If AI is unavailable or a daily quota is reached, it says so. Do not claim a successful reply or retry repeatedly. No source change or re-upload is needed to bypass a save conflict: reload Statements first. Google organisational accounts may have administrator restrictions; try an authorised personal Google account or report the exact access error to the project owner. The API stays private.

Friday cannot send payments, cancel subscriptions, recover money, provide a live balance or guarantee that a transaction is legitimate. Findings are review candidates and may be wrong. It does not offer personalised securities trades.
